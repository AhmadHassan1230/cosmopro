Our process.

Six stages, one team, from the first formula to finished stock.

  1. 01

    Formulation and development

    We develop a new formula or refine yours, based on your requirements.

  2. 02

    Product manufacturing

    Your batch is produced on our floor using reliable, quality-focused processes.

  3. 03

    Filling

    Product is filled accurately and consistently into your bottles, tubes or jars.

  4. 04

    Labelling

    Labels are applied to your artwork and specifications.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Cartons and outer packs complete the product and its presentation.

  6. 06

    Finished product

    Market-ready stock under your brand, prepared for your shelves.
