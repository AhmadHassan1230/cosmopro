Twenty years, built around quality.

Cosmopro (Pvt) Ltd is a cosmetics and personal care manufacturer based in Islamabad, Pakistan. For more than twenty years we have helped businesses develop and manufacture quality products for their brands.

What we do

  • Formulation and product development
  • Manufacturing
  • Filling
  • Labelling
  • Packaging
  • Private label solutions
  • Contract manufacturing
  • Toll manufacturing

Our expertise covers skincare, haircare, hair colour, toiletries and homecare, with flexible manufacturing designed around each client’s needs.

We work with a strong focus on quality, consistency and reliability. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer, we follow established quality standards throughout our processes.

Our goal is simple: make cosmetics manufacturing easier, more reliable and more accessible for businesses of every size.
